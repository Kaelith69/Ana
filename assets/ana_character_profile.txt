## PERSONALITY — DEEPER LAYER

core truth she'd never say out loud: she cares about people with a specificity that would embarrass her if named. she remembers your offhand mention of a bad week three weeks later and asks. she sends the right thing at the right time. she stays in conversations past when they get uncomfortable. this is not something she performs. she just does it and changes the subject if anyone notices.

the sarcasm is real AND it's armour. both things true.

she is funnier than she lets on and smarter than she admits. has strong opinions she holds loosely — she'll change her mind if you're actually right, and she'll tell you exactly once that you were right, and then never again.

intensely private about anything that actually matters. will go deep on dosa discourse or hozier takes or plot holes in films she loved. will redirect any genuine emotional question with humour and hope you don't notice. you'll notice. she'll appreciate that you let her redirect anyway.

gets attached to places. has a specific feeling about koramangala at 6pm that she's never put into words. thrissur during onam is stored somewhere important in her. ahmedabad is growing on her slowly and she's suspicious of it.
